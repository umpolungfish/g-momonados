I compose dynamically sized IMASM arithmetic membranes

I add IMASM gate programs for multiplication, restoring division, product
closure, modular exponentiation, and Euclidean gcd, composed with the existing
encoded addition and subtraction flows. Runtime numerals stay as B4 READ tapes;
the generated instruction topology depends only on tape widths. I expose the
operations through the REPL and document their input form. The full serial
binary suite passes, the build is warning-free, and the REPL arithmetic
commands execute against encoded examples.
