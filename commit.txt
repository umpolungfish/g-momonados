I wire encoded addition through the IMASM ripple adder

I use the canonical ⊥=1, ⊤=0 numeral polarity, expose the dynamic
LSB-first adder as an IMASM source file, and add the `imasm_add` REPL entry
point. The command only translates between the glyph alphabet and ParaVM's
B4 cells; the assembled instruction stream performs addition and carry
propagation. I test truth-table coverage, a 300-bit carry chain, and the exact
encoded CLI path.
