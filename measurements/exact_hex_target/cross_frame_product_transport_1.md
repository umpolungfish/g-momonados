# Cross-frame positioned-product transport

Equal signed corrections identify equal positioned products from different
source framings. Their alternative operand decompositions share the component
catalog. An observed component from one placement is compared with the other
framing's quotient through native divmod; every complete comparison equation is
independently reconstructed. A zero remainder transports that component into a
larger placement and retains the full source correction.

The seven exact comparison paths give five distinct source equations:
`N=9*A+1`, `N=645*B+7`, `N=6141*C+1687`, `N=10235*D+1687`,
and `N=30705*E+1687`. Letters designate the actual returned cofactor words;
their complete values and parent widths are in
`cross_frame_product_transport_1_returns.md`, with native and ordered hex
readings in the corresponding `.log.gz`.

The 645 and 30705 equations each have two parent paths yielding the identical
cofactor word. All transported source equations retain nonzero corrections.
The expanded catalog supplies no proper complete source collection, including
the replay of every width-pair overlay. Its 893 nonempty overlay collections
retain the original source and a unit operand.

This is a finite continuation of the parent's source-origin frames, not a claim
of saturation over every possible signed rewrite or every starting offset.
