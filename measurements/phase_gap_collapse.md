# Fermat square-frontier collapse

For the carried Fermat coordinate

\[
D=a^2-N,\qquad b=\lfloor\sqrt D\rfloor,\qquad g=(b+1)^2-D,
\]

positive `N` gives `b < a`. Therefore

\[
g\le 2b+1<2a+1=\delta.
\]

Every positive phase movement reaches at least one square boundary. A destination can be rejected by the CRT residue image, but a QR-admitted destination can never be rejected as gap-safe in this coordinate. The gap predicate remains inside the tape scheduler and the separate tape counters make this exact boundary visible.

The 511-bit, 128-phase control produced two QR-admitted leaves, 126 CRT-false phases, zero gap-false phases, one carried gap crossing, and two root invocations. Its forty-process median was 3.11 ms.

The 1023-bit, 512-phase control produced five QR-admitted leaves, 507 CRT-false phases, zero gap-false phases, four carried gap crossings, and five root invocations. Its forty-process median was 5.84 ms.

The next reducible cost is consequently the root morphism at QR-admitted destinations.
